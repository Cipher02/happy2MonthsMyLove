My Love, these two months

Ma,

Happy two months, My Love. 💜

I still think about that ‘I LOVE YOU’ at 10:53, and hearing us say it again on the call at 11:01.

Since then, there have been so many things I’ve wanted to keep.

The way we ran into each other’s arms after joking about keeping one metre apart. My first kiss, which I got to share with you. Holding each other in the cold. Your hand finding mine.

And all the little things between those moments.

The stories on our calls. Your teasing. The random ‘I love you’ that makes an ordinary part of my day feel special. Looking at you for a little too long because I’m still admiring how beautiful you are.

I think about the things you chose for me, too.

The shirt you pictured me wearing to temple. Your favourite pen. The little capybara you built. The cookies you were secretly making while telling me you were cleaning the kitchen.

Ma, I felt so thought of.

And when I couldn’t find Twiggies and you bought four for me, it reminded me how carefully you listen to even the smallest things I say.

I want you to feel that same thoughtfulness from me.

I want to keep learning what makes you feel loved, keep asking about your day, and give you my attention when you’re telling me something that matters.

We’ve had days when things felt a little tangled, too. I want us to keep talking kindly, apologising when we need to, and finding our way back to understanding each other. When I need time to think, I want to explain that clearly and let you know when we can talk again.

You can tell me when you need a little more affection, Ma. I want to hear you.

I love your playful side. I also want you to feel comfortable with me when you’re tired, quiet, or simply having a difficult day.

These two months have held so much: visits, goodbyes, calls, little surprises, and your news that you passed your nursing exam. Thank you for letting me share those parts of your life.

I keep remembering how peaceful I felt with my head on your lap.

I’m looking forward to holding you again. Until then, I want to keep making room for us in the days we have now.

More little stories. More laughter. More honest conversations. More forehead kisses whenever I can reach you.

Happy two months, My Love.

I’m so glad we get to keep discovering what loving each other can look like.
